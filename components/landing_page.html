About Delhi Customs

Delhi Customs Zone stands as a guardian of the nation's economic frontiers, ensuring strict compliance with the Customs Act, 1962. We are dedicated to the twin goals of Trade Facilitation and Enforcement, streamlining cross-border movement of goods and passengers while robustly preventing smuggling and commercial fraud.

Through the implementation of modern, technology-driven initiatives like Turant Customs and Faceless Assessment, we strive to enhance the ease of doing business, ensuring transparency, efficiency, and accountability in all our operations for the benefit of stakeholders and the nation.

Sh. Samanjasa Das

Principal Chief Commissioner,
Chief Commissioner Office

"Delhi Customs emphasizes that trade facilitation and border security together ensure the economic welfare of our nation. We are committed to providing efficient, transparent services to all stakeholders while robustly enforcing the laws of the land."

Security Alert: Be Vigilant

Beware of fake calls, SMSs, or emails asking for duty payments into personal accounts.
Indian Customs NEVER asks for money in individual bank accounts. All duty payments are to be made ONLY through the official portal (icegate.gov.in) or appointed banks. If you receive such a request, report it immediately.
